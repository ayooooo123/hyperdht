Observed invariant gap
----------------------
A review-only hook between genuine activation prepare and commit changes one
material property. The peer commit accepts changes to localDeadline,
tailControl, and clockIdentity. The unchanged control also commits.
The material object is not frozen.

Scope and limitation
--------------------
This is an internal fault-injection proof of absent commit-time metadata
validation, NOT a demonstrated remote exploit or proof that an ordinary caller
can interpose in the synchronous claim path. The probe instruments only the
shared claim orchestration to insert the mutation. It does not fabricate
handoffs, reservations, activation owners, peer runtimes, or network authority.
It restores metadata after observing the result for normal owner cleanup.
